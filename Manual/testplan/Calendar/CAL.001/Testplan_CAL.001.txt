# CTO-flagged frequent-breakage area (raised 2026-05-07): Book Appointment
# is the highest-business-risk surface in the suite — a wrong-time booking
# means the customer shows up without a practitioner and the clinic loses
# trust. Failure classes called out by the CTO:
#   • Double bookings (same slot booked twice — race condition or missing
#     server-side slot lock)
#   • Wrong-time bookings (timezone / DST / drag-resize / click-to-time bug)
#   • Confirmation emails not received (SMTP / queue / template / recipient
#     resolution failure)
